When Duolite® Shades Fit Your Home

Selecting window coverings involves balancing aesthetic preferences with practical needs like light control and privacy. Different shade technologies offer distinct solutions, often at varying price points, requiring careful consideration of daily use and room function.

Consider a common situation: a homeowner is renovating their living room and is torn between two window shade options for their Rocklin home. They love the idea of having both sheer and blackout capabilities in one Duolite® shade but also finds the independent top and bottom movement of top-down shades appealing for privacy and light. They wonder if the added cost of Duolite® is worth the enhanced light flexibility. Should the household prioritize the dual-layer light control of Duolite® shades or the versatile top-down, bottom-up functionality for their Rocklin home?

Recommendation: Duolite® Shades

Blind Spot recommends Duolite® shades for homeowners seeking maximum flexibility in light management. This option is ideal when you need to transition a room from bright daylight to a completely dark environment without switching window treatments.
