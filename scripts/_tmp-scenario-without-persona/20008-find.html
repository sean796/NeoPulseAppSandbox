A Realistic Gold River Installation Scenario

Older homes often present unique challenges for window treatments due to original construction and materials. Finding solutions that fit precisely and enhance energy efficiency can be difficult.

Brenda is struggling to decide on the best window treatments for her Gold River, Rancho Cordova home. The original single-pane aluminum windows from the 1970s make standard options a poor fit, and she's unsure whether custom shutters, blinds, or shades would be the most effective solution. Should Brenda opt for custom shutters, blinds, or shades for her home's unique window types?

Recommendation: Custom Shutters

Blind Spot would recommend custom shutters when a classic aesthetic and improved insulation are priorities, as they can be precisely fitted to older, non-standard window frames. This provides an effective window covering solution that respects the home's original style.
