FAQ

To help you keep your window treatments looking their best, we’ve compiled answers to some common questions about how to clean fabric blinds safely and effectively.

Question Answer
How can I remove dust from fabric blinds without causing damage? Use a vacuum cleaner with a soft brush attachment set to a low suction power. Gently go over the surface of the fabric blinds to lift dust particles. This method is effective for most fabric types and helps prevent heavy grime buildup.
What should I do if my fabric blinds have minor spot stains? For small stains, test a mild detergent solution mixed with water on an inconspicuous area first. If the fabric is colorfast, gently blot the stain with a clean, damp cloth. Avoid aggressive scrubbing, which can harm delicate materials.
When is professional cleaning the best option for fabric blinds? Professional cleaning is recommended for delicate fabrics like silk or linen, or when dealing with widespread or stubborn stains. Expert services use specialized techniques and solutions to clean safely and effectively, preventing potential damage from DIY attempts.
How does the local climate in the Edmonton area affect cleaning needs? The local climate can influence how quickly dust accumulates and how humidity affects fabric fibers, potentially leading to mildew. Increased sunlight can also cause fading. These factors mean gentle, regular cleaning is important to maintain the blinds’ appearance and integrity.
