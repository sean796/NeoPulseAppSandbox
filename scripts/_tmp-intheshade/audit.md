# **In The Shade: Content Audit**

Blog posts and service-area pages on intheshadeflorida.com. Audited 15 November 2026 by Neo Digital.

## **Summary**

Most of the blog posts and service-area pages contain incorrect or misleading information and need correcting before they can be relied on. The audit found 946 issues on 210 of the 212 pages, and 114 of them are high severity, spread over 94 pages.  
All 212 pages were read in full: 56 blog posts and 156 service-area pages, including 13 scheduled posts and 2 drafts. Each page was checked against the business details, the Hunter Douglas product line In The Shade sells, and Florida geography. Nothing on the site was changed.

## **Main problems**

> 1. **Pages for towns outside the service area.** 54 published pages and 2 drafts target towns 48 to 394 miles from Stuart, including Panama City, Apalachicola, Blountstown, Bristol, Parker, Alachua, the Orlando area, Tampa and Clearwater, Miami-Dade and Broward. About 20 of them invent local history, such as having installed hundreds of window treatments in Alachua.  
> 2. **Services the business does not list.** Repair, including emergency repair and price tables, appears on 31 pages. Exterior, hurricane or impact shutters, or interior shutters described as storm protection, appear on 22\. Ready-made curtains appear on 17, retrofitting motors onto existing blinds on 11, and whole-home automation on 7\. These need confirming with the client; they are not proven false.  
> 3. **Wrong contact and showroom details.** The Opa Locka page gives the phone number 772-220-4949, which is wrong. One page describes a showroom near Palm City, and others make similar showroom claims. Only the Stuart and Port St. Lucie showrooms exist.  
> 4. **Product errors.** 87 pages have product mistakes. 24 describe SoftTouch as app, Alexa or internet controlled, or call it Somfy SoftTouch. Others name competitor brands (Graber, Norman, Levolor, Polybilt), refer to LightLock roller shades, call Silhouette a blind, mention the discontinued Applause and Solera lines, invent warranty terms, or say PowerView controls lights and climate.  
> 5. **First six blog posts shifted by one.** The web address and FAQ data of each of these posts belong to the previous topic. For example, the page at /blog/what-are-aura-illuminated-shades/ holds the Hunter Douglas guide, and the solar chargers post no longer exists.  
> 6. **FAQ answers that say nothing.** 71 pages answer each FAQ by repeating the question. 37 pages say they serve the United States, Canada and Mexico.  
> 7. **Template leftovers.** More than 26 pages have the page title pasted where the town name should be. There are 7 pairs of pages with the same title, 4 Apalachicola pages (one with a misspelled address), and some garbled text.  
> 8. **Wrong places and sources.** 44 pages name the wrong place or cite an unrelated source: Rio confused with Rio Vista Isles in Fort Lauderdale, Orlando and Oviedo on Treasure Coast posts, a San Diego Wikipedia link on Stuart and Palm City pages, and a Washington State PDF.  
> 9. **Internal links that go to the wrong page.** On 143 pages the link text does not match where the link goes. All published links do load.

Motorized shutters were not counted as errors, because Hunter Douglas offers PowerView on shutters.
