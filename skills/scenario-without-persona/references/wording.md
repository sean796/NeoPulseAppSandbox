# Situation wording (no invented first name)

## Allowed blockquote openers (vary per page)

- In this scenario
- Consider a common situation
- For example
- In most cases

Genuine job notes from a real source may use "in a recent installation" with no invented name.

## Body pattern

1. Intro paragraph: general decision context. No first name. No question.
2. Blockquote: opener + situation with "a homeowner", "their", or "the household".
3. Recommendation h3 + site-first paragraph naming the connected business.

## Forbidden

- Inventing Nadia, Arthur, Anya, Maya, Olivia, Quinn, Elena, Eleanor, or any other proper first name as a customer or homeowner.
- "{Name} is/has/needs…" homeowner patterns.
- Requiring the same opener on every page.

## Keep

- Hunter Douglas, Alta, Duette, PowerView, SoftTouch, and other product lines
- Paul Kane Park, James Macdonald Bridge, and other real place names
- "Mark Outside-Mount" and similar instruction labels
- Blind Magic (business name)
